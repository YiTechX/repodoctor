# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want RepoDoctor to be a place where anyone can contribute, whatever their experience level,
background, identity or location. Everyone who takes part in this project agrees to help keep it
welcoming, respectful and harassment-free.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was a beginner once, and a patient answer today creates a
  maintainer tomorrow.
- Respect other people's time. Search existing issues before opening a new one.
- Credit the work of others.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or unwelcome attention.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any
public place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions and temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. To report a problem, use the private vulnerability
reporting form in the Security tab of the repository, or contact a maintainer through the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided
by the other maintainers.
